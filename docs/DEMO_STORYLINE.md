# Tableau Pulse Demo Storyline: The Carrera Racing Championship

## 🎬 Demo Overview (5-10 minutes)

This demo tells the story of 6 months at a Carrera Slot Car Racing track, where corporate teams compete at exhibitions and practice sessions. Through this narrative, we naturally showcase all Tableau Pulse capabilities.

**Hook**: "Let me show you how Tableau Pulse helped us understand performance patterns at our racing track, uncovering insights we never would have found manually."

---

## 📖 The Story Arc (Timeline)

### Act 1: Setting the Scene (Weeks 1-5)
**The Setup**: Corporate racing league with 128 drivers, 13 cars, regular competitions

### Act 2: The Game Changer (Week 6)
**The Crisis**: Track reconfiguration disrupts everything

### Act 3: Adaptation & Discovery (Weeks 7-12+)
**The Resolution**: Drivers adapt, patterns emerge, champions rise

### Scene 2: "The Week 6 Mystery" (2 minutes)
**Showcase: Trend Change Alert & Unexpected Values**

**Narrative**:
> "In early July, something dramatic happened. Let's ask Pulse what changed..."

**Action**:
1. Point to **Trend Change Alert** notification for Week 6
2. Click to see details: "Lap times increased 10% suddenly"
3. Ask Enhanced Q&A: **"What happened to lap times in week 6?"**

**Expected AI Response**:
> "In week 6, lap times increased significantly across all drivers, showing an average increase of 10%. This affected every participant equally, suggesting a track-level change rather than driver performance issues. The pattern reversed over the following 2 weeks as drivers adapted."

**Tableau Pulse Features Highlighted**:
- ✓ Trend Change Alert (automatic detection)
- ✓ Unexpected Values insight
- ✓ Enhanced Q&A (conversational analysis)
- ✓ Common Contributors (all drivers affected)

**Key Talking Point**:
> "Without building a single chart, Pulse detected the track reconfiguration, identified it affected everyone equally, and even noticed the adaptation period. This is the power of AI-driven insights."

## 🎭 Complete Demo Script (Verbatim)

### Opening (30 seconds)

> "I'm going to show you 6 months of data from our Carrera Slot Car Racing track. We have 128 drivers from various companies competing in exhibitions and practice sessions. Instead of showing you dashboards, I'm going to let Tableau Pulse tell us the story.
>

### The Week 6 Discovery (90 seconds)

> "We started tracking in June. Everything looked normal - drivers getting faster as they learned the track. Then in early July..."
>
> [Show Trend Change Alert]
>
> "Pulse automatically flagged this. Something changed dramatically. Let me ask what happened..."
>
> [Type: "What happened to lap times in week 6?"]
>
> "Look at this - Pulse tells me lap times increased 10%, it affected ALL drivers equally, and they adapted over 2 weeks. Turns out we reconfigured the track that week.
>
> Think about this: No one told Pulse about the track change. It detected the pattern, identified it was universal, and even noticed the learning curve. That's the power of AI-driven insights."

### The Equipment Discovery (90 seconds)

> "Now, some drivers started performing better than others. Was it skill or equipment? Let me ask..."
>
> [Type: "Which car model performs best?"]
>
> "Fascinating! The Porsche 911 RSR is 7% faster on average. But here's what's clever..."
>
> [Show usage over time chart]
>
> "Pulse noticed that after week 6, more drivers started requesting this car. It connected two insights: This car IS faster, AND drivers figured it out. That's cross-metric reasoning - finding relationships I might never spot manually."

> "The magic is you can just have a conversation with your data..."
>
> [Rapid fire:]
> - "Compare weekend vs weekday performance" → "2% faster on weekends"
> - "Who improved the most?" → Rankings
> - "Show morning vs evening sessions" → "Morning sessions faster"
>
> "No dashboard required. No filters to configure. Just questions in plain English, answered instantly with AI-generated insights."

### Closing (30 seconds)

> "This is Tableau Pulse. It watches your metrics 24/7, detects changes automatically, explains what's happening in plain language, and lets you explore through conversation.
>
> What took us months to notice manually, Pulse found in seconds. And it scales to thousands of metrics across your entire organization.
>
> Questions?"

## 🎨 Visual Storytelling Tips

### Show, Don't Tell:
- **Week 6**: Let the Trend Change Alert appear naturally, don't explain it first
- **Equipment**: Show the chart AFTER asking the question, not before
- **Consistency**: Compare two drivers side-by-side visually

### Pace Control:
- **Pause** after major insights to let them land
- **Rapid fire** during Q&A to show speed
- **Slow down** for complex cross-metric analysis

### Audience Engagement:
- "What do YOU think caused this?" (before revealing Week 6 answer)
- "Which car would YOU choose?" (before showing equipment data)
- "What would YOU ask next?" (during conversational section)
